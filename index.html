How booking works

  1. Choose villas

    Filter by area, size and facilities, switch between monthly and yearly rent, and shortlist up to three.

  2. Send a request

    Share your move-in date, how long you want to stay and who is moving in. No payment is taken at this stage.

  3. We confirm with the owner

    Our team checks availability, rent and terms with the owner or manager, and can arrange a viewing or video tour.

  4. Sign and move in

    Review the rental agreement, arrange the deposit and first payment, and receive your keys.

BaliStayora is an agency. Villas belong to their owners and partner managers. A rental is only final after you receive written confirmation and a signed agreement.

Questions before you book

Does BaliStayora own the villas?

No. We list and market villas on behalf of owners and partners, and we handle your booking from request to arrival.

Is the rent on the site final?

Listed rents are a guide. They vary with the length of stay and move-in date, and a yearly term is often cheaper per month. We confirm the exact rent with the owner before you commit.

Do I pay when I send a request?

No. You pay only after our team confirms availability and sends the booking terms.

What is the minimum stay?

It depends on the owner. Some villas rent from one month, others only on a yearly term. Each listing shows which terms are offered.

What is included in the rent?

Electricity, Wi-Fi, cleaning and pool care differ by villa. We confirm what is included, and the deposit, in writing before you sign.
